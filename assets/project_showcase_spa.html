TRANSFORMING SOCIALFI

Decentralized Social Tipping Bridge

We are building the first trustless infrastructure that cryptographically links social identities to the Stellar network, enabling seamless micro-transactions across the social web.

Problem Statement

Social identities are currently siloed from financial tools. Existing "tipping bots" are centralized intermediaries that pose significant custodial risks. Users are forced to manually share long wallet addresses, creating high friction and security vulnerabilities.

Fragile Security Custodial Risk User Friction

Our Solution

A non-custodial bridge connecting Telegram and X identities directly to Soroban Smart Contracts. By utilizing decentralized registry principles, we ensure that funds move directly from sender to receiver without any middleman holding the private keys.

100% On-Chain Soroban-Powered Trustless

Market Opportunity: The Micro-Economy

Social tipping requires transaction costs to be lower than the tip itself. Stellar is the only network where sending $0.05 is economically viable, making it the perfect home for SocialFi.

Scalability

Sub-second Confirmation

Tipping should be as instant as a 'Like'. Our bridge processes transactions in under 5 seconds.

Monetization

Creator Independence

Capturing the long-tail of micro-transactions that are currently lost to platform fees or high gas costs.

Architecture & Interoperability

Secure, distributed, and multi-wallet by design.

🔌

Multi-Wallet Connector

Full support for the Stellar ecosystem. Users can connect via Freighter, Albedo, Rabe, or LOBSTR. Our interface automatically detects the best available signer for the browser.

📜

Soroban Registry

Built with Rust for formal security. The registry contract manages the mapping between social IDs and public keys entirely on-chain without an external database.

🛡️

Non-Custodial Flows

Transactions are constructed on the client-side and sent to the network only after the user's wallet signature. The bridge never sees or touches your private keys.
